Cultural Logic

Decision Architecture for Indian Celebrations

Generic planners treat budgets as flat percentages. CelebrationAI is built around the mathematical and cultural realities of Indian family gatherings.

Catering Scales Per Head

Fixed costs like decor and photography stay constant, while live food counters scale with every single guest plate.

Concrete Example: At 350 guests, catering takes 32% (₹5,76,000 at ₹1,645/head). Increasing to 500 guests automatically expands catering to 36% (₹6,48,000 at ₹1,296/head) to protect multi-meal buffet standards.

Muhurat-Aware Timeline

Milestones calculate backwards from your date, automatically compressing lead times if your celebration falls in peak muhurat season.

Concrete Example: For an event 45 days away, standard 150-day venue locks are compressed into a 48-hour urgent action tag with immediate caterer tasting alerts.

Vendor Lead Times by Season

Prime Indian wedding dates between November and February book out 6 to 9 months in advance across major tier-1 and tier-2 cities.

Concrete Example: Banquet halls and top bridal makeup artists demand 150-day lead times during peak season, compared to 45-day lead times for monsoon celebrations.

Budget Realism Check

Automated thresholds detect unrealistic budgets per guest before you sign non-refundable vendor advances.

Concrete Example: A wedding budget under ₹1,200/guest or 4+ functions under ₹2,500/head triggers an immediate advisory suggesting you host Haldi at home to save ₹60,000 in venue rent.
